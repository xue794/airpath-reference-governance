Supplement S11 supporting data
Air-Path Reference Governance for Fixed-Wing Tracking under Wind

These machine-readable records accompany Supplement S11. They contain existing numerical results, including interval trees, paired performance measures and event data. The four new simulations following the prescribed center are identified separately as a post hoc comparison. Values in the article and supplement are rounded for display.

S11_1_screening/
Candidate counterexamples, force and backup-row residuals, historical event times, feedback error boxes and pointwise evaluations. A negative backup derivative slack has the units of the corresponding row's time derivative. It is distinct from the endpoint force gap in N.

S11_2_certificate/
The ellipsoid uses a 22 s center descent to 31.25 m/s and level 0.01. The nominal certificate spans 0–55 s; feedback results are conditional on the specified tracking errors during 14–20 s. See Supplement S11.2 for error and attitude hypotheses. The aggregate verified bounds and the location of each worst retained leaf appear in all_bounds.csv. Files with nominal_focused, directional_focused, uniform_focused and joint are the final four partition trees. For each tree, the JSON gives initial time slabs and coordinate projections, and the review file gives the checked bounds. In *_leaves.csv.gz the slab index is zero based; the root heap path is 1; left and right children have indices 2p and 2p+1. An intermediate class is the axis of bisection (0: time; 1 through 5: speed, climb angle, speed rate, climb rate, course rate). Outside leaves are excluded by the ellipsoid test. Preliminary searches ending at budget are retained as summaries in preliminary/ and are not used as positive certificates. The uniform-box counterexample and the attitude hypothesis matter when interpreting J. In decision.json's directional_focused block, payLower and payUpper are lower bounds on 10 N minus the signed reference-to-feedback loss; fbLo and fbHi are lower bounds on the raw feedback gaps. In the joint block rawTmax denotes upper-limit slack (60 N minus the raw feedback thrust).

S11_3_development/ and S11_3_confirmation/
D0 denotes the 8 s development ramp. C1, C2, and C3 are independent prescribed confirmation settings; the step labels 010 and 005 represent the maximum ODE step in milliseconds (10 and 5 ms). O is the original static backup, F the moving backup with both 10 N endpoint rows, D the same tube with those rows removed, A the same tube with reserve-matched angle rows. T, N10 and Am denote the no-backup-row thrust, force-endpoint and matched-angle comparisons, respectively. D0 and C1 include these three arms. The four root margins are ordered reference lower, reference upper, feedback lower, feedback upper. Events identifies the first original root boundary or the upstream command-chart exit. Reference thrust, raw feedback demand and actual thrust are separate fields. A small signed roundoff at a located boundary represents zero. Common-window comparisons end at the last shared sample; complete-window statistics use runs covering the whole indicated window. For F, matched_replay records same-state substitutions and stationarity/feasibility checks. free_replay in the confirmation folder records substitutions on C1/N10 states.

S11_5_direct_center/
Two independent integrations per request compare the exact analytic center with F using the same aircraft and downstream control law. No wind or noise was applied to C1 or C2. Actual and reference groundspeed MAE use trapezoidal integration over 3–16 s; the platform means use 25–35 s. Position RMS follows the original three-dimensional sampled definition. In paired_differences.csv a positive Direct_minus_F means larger values for direct following: better tracking by F for an error metric, greater remaining margin for direct following for a force-gap metric. speed_comparison.csv reports the descriptive ratio (O minus Direct)/(O minus F) for actual groundspeed MAE. A step refinement is a numerical check, not a new independent flight scenario. The empty events table for the direct comparison means no monitored boundary crossing.

figure9/
Selected fine-step C1 histories for the figure and mechanism comparison, in seconds, m/s, N, m, or degrees as indicated below. vground and vrequest are horizontal ground speeds, vref_ground is reference horizontal ground speed, refLo/refHi and fbLo/fbHi are the lower/upper reference and feedback gaps (N), Tref/Traw/Tact are thrusts (N), positionError is the distance to the requested position (m), eV is the airspeed error (m/s), and egamma_deg/echi_deg/theta_sum_deg are angular errors (degrees). The files retain each arm's original observed time span.

SHA256SUMS.txt gives SHA-256 digests of the enclosed data and guide. CSV and JSON contain decimal point numbers; some source CSV files include a UTF-8 BOM.

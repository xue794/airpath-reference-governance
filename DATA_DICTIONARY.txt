Data conventions

Methods: Proposed = Scalar; MatchedQP = Matched-QP; Transparent = unmodified
nominal reference. Deceleration arms O = original static backup, F = moving
backup with full rows, D = endpoint deletion inside the moving backup,
A = matched angle rows inside the moving backup, T = thrust/conventional
rows with no backup, N10 = 10 N endpoint reserve with no backup,
Am = force-matched angle protection with no backup, Direct = center following.

Time is in seconds. Suffix 005 means maximum integration step 0.005 s;
010 means 0.01 s. Saved output grids are generally 0.05 s. Counts refer to
saved points and do not establish continuous-time trajectory guarantees.

PositionRMS_grid_m / PosRMSFull_m / position_RMS: square root of the arithmetic
mean of squared saved position errors, in metres. Full-horizon quantities
are not assigned to an incomplete requested horizon. Common-prefix metrics
use the separately declared intersection window.

Deceleration speed error: integral of abs(vground-vrequest) over 3-16 s,
computed by the trapezoidal rule on the saved grid and divided by 13 s.
Plateau means use 25-35 s. Feedback-gap minima use 14-20 s where indicated.
Angles with _deg suffix are degrees; implementation angles are radians.
Force gaps and thrust are N. reference_T_N / Tref refers to the nominal
reference inversion, raw_T_N / Traw to feedback before actuator clipping,
actuator_T_N / Tact to the modeled applied actuator state.

VB_over_rho > 1 indicates membership outside the static certified ellipsoid;
it is not itself a physical actuator or aircraft limit violation. Membership
and margin-transfer assumptions are separate from invariant tracking bounds.

NaN or blank values indicate unavailable/not-applicable values (often an
incomplete horizon); do not replace them with zero. Failure classes and
completion flags remain in the source tables, including all 500 pairs.
Positive dPosRMSFull_m means QP minus Scalar and favors Scalar on that metric.

Timing records are block-average per-call costs, not individual-call samples.
The reported whole-governor reduction is a ratio of marginal medians, distinct
from a median of the within-block ratios. Historical source paths in some
tables identify provenance; the release map supplies their local counterpart.
